# EXO Sample 3D — cloth simulation research and implementation agenda

**Scope:** research proposals and existing evidence as of 15 October 2026. This document concerns numerical cloth mechanics, not the material appearance renderer. Its claims are bounded by the reference fixtures and run reports cited below. It does not certify any garment as safe or manufacturable.

## Research questions

The product needs a credible answer to five questions: how accurately does a pattern become a sewn manifold; how does the fabric deform under load and motion; when are two moving surfaces truly collision-free; how sensitive is the answer to mesh and time discretization; and how closely do simulated clearances and shapes predict a measured real garment? A visually plausible frame alone answers none of these reliably. A comparison to the JavaScript baseline measures implementation fidelity, while a comparison to physical experiment measures modeling fidelity. Preserve both evidence channels in every report.

Research already exists in [next generation simulation](GARMENT_SIMULATION_NEXT_GEN.md), [PPF on-body integration](ZOZO_PPF_ON_BODY_INTEGRATION_2026-10-13.md), [actual fit deep audit](PPF_ACTUAL_GARMENT_FIT_DEEP_AUDIT_2026-10-14.md), [swept contact integration](IPC_THOMAS_SWEPT_CONTACT_INTEGRATION_2026-10-13.md) and [PPF contact diagnostics](PPF_CONTACT_SAFETY_NEXT_PASS_2026-10-13.md). Some reports document failed or narrowly passing experiments. Keep these failures intact; they are regression fixtures rather than evidence to suppress.

## Mathematical model and units

Represent each garment as a set of oriented surface triangles with rest-space 2D coordinates, current 3D positions, per-vertex masses, garment-to-avatar alignment and explicit stitch constraints. In a conventional finite-element treatment, the membrane response follows the deformation gradient on each triangle; stretching and shearing penalties depend on orientation relative to warp and weft. Bending couples adjacent faces through dihedral angle or an equivalent discrete curvature. An XPBD scheme uses compliant constraints and Lagrange multipliers whose effective stiffness depends on timestep and the compliance formulation. Document gravity in m/s², vertex position in meters, thickness in meters, fabric mass in kg/m², force in newtons, stretch stiffness with a named constitutive convention, and friction as a dimensionless coefficient.

An integration step should define an ordering such as force accumulation, predicted position, seam constraints, body contact, self-contact, velocity/previous-position synchronization, and final diagnostics. Ordering matters; matching final positions while prior positions diverge can create different next-frame velocities and friction. The existing `scripts/check-native-cpp-cloth-solver-step-contacts.mjs` is relevant to one-frame cross-language comparison, including earlier position mismatch investigations. Record both previous and current coordinates, accumulated constraint corrections, contact depths, contact normals and contact counts at named stages. Extend diagnostics without changing solver behavior in the act of measuring it.

The backend API should include `solverVersion`, integration method, fixed time step, substeps, iteration counts, mesh counts, thickness, compliant material coefficients, friction law, collision mode, backend capabilities and explicit initialization provenance. Distinguish a reset from continuation of a warmed simulation. A physical fit result must cite all of these, plus its avatar pose and garment pattern hash.

## Rust PPF role and native boundaries

The optional Rust-based PPF contact service is documented in [Rust backend](RUST_BACKEND.md). Upstream `st-tech/ppf-contact-solver` resides in ignored local research folders and must not be assumed to be redistributable as an EXO binary. The web workspace may connect to a separately launched local service; `npm run ppf:serve` and `npm run ppf:health` are useful development entry points. The C++ code's involvement is an adapter and a source of independently validated modeling operations. A hybrid C++ plus Rust engine is a sound maintainable target when a versioned interchange schema and controlled solver failure behavior exist.

Define a wire format with immutable rest mesh, stitch graph, dynamic state and run settings as distinct objects. Every array must have a declared indexing basis and endianness; every field must carry units or be defined by schema. Hash topology, positions, material fields and avatar motion to detect stale results. Reject mismatched counts, nonfinite coordinates, negative mass, inverted faces and unsupported capabilities before submitting to a solver. The service should return structured errors, frame-by-frame statistics and trajectory checkpoints; it must not infer success simply because a request returned HTTP 200. Study `scripts/check-native-cpp-ppf-payload.mjs`, `scripts/check-ppf-payload.mjs`, `scripts/check-ppf-multirun-robustness.mjs`, and [native PPF integration checkpoint](NATIVE_PPF_INTEGRATION_CHECKPOINT_2026-10-14.md).

When a particular device lacks the required solver, provide an honest browser preview status. Avoid substituting an animated morph or precomputed motion for a solved fit without labeling it. If a Rust sidecar crashes, keep the authored project safe, preserve the last known diagnostic record and let the user restart deliberately.

## Discrete body contact versus continuous collision detection

Discrete penetration correction tests body intersections at one sampled time. CCD tests whether moving triangles, vertices or edges crossed within a time interval, even if their endpoints are separated. A vertex moving from the front to the back of an arm between frames may show zero penetration at both endpoints and still tunnel through the avatar. Any claim of CCD requires conservative temporal checks, contact normal accuracy, degeneracy handling and robust treatment of edge-edge and vertex-face events, including near-coplanar and tangential motion.

The research plan should compare at least three representations: primitive avatar proxies, triangle-mesh nearest-surface contact, and swept triangle interactions. Measure the gap between proxy and visible surface, especially near armpits, crotch, shoulder transitions and thin wrists. Sample both the simulated garment and reference avatar at each interpolated pose. Set separate error limits for the shape proxy and for actual cloth-body penetration. Tests for body correction include `scripts/check-native-cpp-avatar-surface-real-fixtures.mjs`, `scripts/check-native-cpp-mesh-avatar-contact.mjs`, `scripts/check-native-cpp-avatar-contact-kernel.mjs` and `scripts/check-ppf-upstream-motion-contact.mjs`.

Do not equate a reduced mean penetration depth with zero geometric intersections. Record maximum signed penetration, total penetrating vertices, penetrating area, sustained-duration contacts and positive clearance distribution. Correct normal orientation and consistent units are essential to all of these. Persist failure snapshots that a developer can inspect frame by frame.

## Self-collision and multilayer garments

Self-contact requires broad-phase candidate generation, adjacent-triangle exclusion, continuous vertex-face and edge-edge tests, contact response without mesh inversion, and an approach to intersections already present in initial conditions. Collisions between layers require stable sidedness or an explicit two-sided collision policy. A folded cuff, rolled waistband and sleeve crossing the torso test distinct mechanisms; they cannot be represented by a generic pair of widely separated planar cloth samples.

Use spatial hashing or a BVH over swept bounds, with deterministic candidate ordering for repeatability. Exclude triangles sharing topological edges while considering whether vertices connected only through distant seam constraints should still collide. Preserve a physically defensible minimum separation related to cloth thickness; avoid forcing unrealistic gaps just to eliminate red heat-map pixels. Observe contact jitter, energy injection, seam drift and friction-induced sticking across hundreds of frames. Compare the native fixtures in `scripts/check-native-cpp-self-collision-real-fixtures.mjs` and `scripts/check-native-cpp-cloth-collision-step.mjs` with independent Rust trajectories. No self-contact feature is accepted solely on a single successful tee pose.

## Sewing, easing and joint contact

A fabric panel begins as a two-dimensional manifold. Sewing joins boundary arcs through a carefully defined sequence: validate compatible arc direction, resample comparable locations, apply optional easing distribution, construct seam constraints, initialize panels in a non-intersecting arrangement and gradually close stitches while detecting body and cloth collisions. For unmatched lengths, the algorithm must distinguish intended gathering from bad design. A perfect coincidence of seam vertices is not necessary during intermediate iterations, but residual mismatch must be measured and bounded.

Advanced options such as contact-constrained sewing and joint-constraint modes need dedicated fixtures for T-shirt armholes, trouser inseams and zipper junctions. Sample iterations 0, 1, 4 and 6, multiple sewing passes, varying contact margin and weighted virtual zipper nodes. Compare all returned metrics and mutated particle states, including incomplete convergence behavior. See `scripts/check-native-cpp-seam-assembly-parity-bridge.mjs`, [nonrigid assembly](SEW_FIRST_NONRIGID_ASSEMBLY_2026-10-13.md) and [physical sewing implementation](PPF_PHYSICAL_SEWING_IMPLEMENTATION_2026-10-13.md). Retain an exact failing setup in the corpus when any algorithm ends with fused or penetrated sleeves.

## Material calibration and model validity

Make a physical swatch library with a source record for each fabric. Acquire mass-per-area, thickness, directional tensile curves, shear response, bending behavior and friction measurements from a defined protocol. Distinguish garment wash treatments, humidity, bias orientation and anisotropy. Record uncertainty: one unverified nominal stiffness should not be treated as a precisely measured constitutive constant. Map this data into both preview and acceptance solvers with explicit approximation errors. The appearance presets described in [fabric audit](SAMPL3D_FABRIC_PARITY_AUDIT_2026-10-15.md) can be used for display without promoting them to measured mechanical presets.

For validation, hang rectangular swatches, drape over a cylinder, bend a strip, and simulate a known garment on a known support. Scan or photograph calibrated experiment outlines, measure landmark coordinates and compare shape, folds and transient motion rather than a single attractive still. Repeat measurements for alternative mesh resolution and timestep. Prefer measurement confidence bands over arbitrary decimal-place targets.

## Research experiment matrix

For each cloth fixture, vary triangle edge length, timestep, substeps, solver iterations, collision thickness, friction and motion speed. Save an experiment grid of input hashes, solver version, hardware, wall time, memory consumption, contact rates, maximum penetration, seam residual, simulated energy and shape error. Analyze convergence rather than selecting the prettiest run. Define stable defaults only after observing sensitivity, and publish the failure region as part of the supported envelope.

Recommended experiments are an isolated falling patch, a pinned rectangle, two approaching cloth squares, a rotated grain swatch, a sleeve being sewn around an articulated shoulder, a trouser leg crossing a bent knee, cloth sliding over a hip, a layered hood and shell, and a fast sweeping arm under a fitted tee. Each includes zero-motion controls and deliberate invalid inputs. A performance target is meaningful only after the physical error budget is fixed: faster but silently interpenetrating cloth is not an optimization success.

## Research acceptance and publication

Three levels of evidence must remain separate. **Implementation parity** demonstrates a native implementation agrees with an identified reference in tested fields. **Numerical validity** demonstrates stability, conservation or bounded dissipative behavior, convergence and robust handling of pathological geometry. **Physical validity** demonstrates reproducible agreement with measured fabric and fit observations. Report all three independently. For each experiment, archive source, setup, metric definitions, failing and passing trajectories and screenshots with the same camera/lighting when used for comparison.

Only after swept-contact and full-motion measurements pass the gates in [validation plan](EXO_VALIDATION_TEST_PLAN.md) should a build display an unqualified on-body simulation acceptance label. A PPF service can remain a valuable experimental backend long before that gate is reached.

## User brief
User dislikes long invented fashion-tech brands, prefers Blender-like software naming, liked EXO and Vestra, and now asks for names containing **sample**, **prototype/proto**, or a garment/clothing reference. Preserve Garment Studio app source name until a final explicit brand decision.

## Research completed
- Screened 756 distinct .com candidates directly through GoDaddy's exact domain availability endpoint in five batches on October 14, 2026.
- 470 were returned available, 286 not available or not returned available.
- Rechecked 41 finalists directly in an additional call. Availability and GoDaddy retail / introductory prices are momentary, subject to checkout changes.
- Standard .com display: initial $12.99; list $22.99. Verify renewal and registration terms at checkout.
- Web-searched exact names to screen obvious conflicts; no comprehensive trademarks, social handles, geographic common law, or legal opinion.

## Research nuances and risks

- **Toile** is actual high-fashion technical vocabulary for test garment, often in inexpensive muslin fabric. Sources: https://www.wearview.co/glossary/toile and https://fashion-incubator.com/the-13-different-kinds-of-samples/
- **ProtoCloth** term also exists on a Mercier Prototype watch listing as a strap material, see https://mercierprototype.com/product-manx-blanc.html ; this is not evidence of trademark registration, but shows it's not purely unique.
- **ExoProto** is used as a costume set designation in game City of Heroes; see https://cityofheroes.fandom.com/wiki/Costume_Sets
- **Samplino** is already a font-family name and knitting pattern title; deprioritize despite available domain.
- **SampleForm** common programming/demo term; existing uses in various software libraries.
- **ToileLab** overlap with Japanese toilet emergency supplies product named TOILELAB; not competitive but may interfere with search visibility.
- Preliminary exact web queries didn't reveal established fashion-CAD brands for Samplory / Samplry / ProtoDrape / ProtoVesti / Garmetra, but this is not sufficient for trademark clearance.
- Prioritize distinctiveness, ease of spelling, and future expansion over just domain price.
- Before registering or using commercially: review https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search and https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/ ; also search international trademarks and unregistered brand use.

## Preliminary ranking

1. **Samplory** — strongest Blender-like independent creative brand under standard .com price.

3. **ExoSample** — best continuation of previous user's EXO naming direction.
4. **ToileForm** — strongest insider apparel term; potential pronunciation and toilet-confusion issues.
5. **ProtoVesti** — timeless and garment-rooted.
6. **SampleForm** — clean professional name, but costs $880 and generic programming usage.

No domain purchased. No app rebrand or code changes performed.
